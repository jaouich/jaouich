Lesson 3: Check before you trust

How to spot wrong answers, invented sources and out-of-date facts before they cost you.

What you will learn

  • Red flags
  • The 3-step check
  • Best sources
  • Be extra careful with

Transcript

Lesson three. The most important skill with AI is checking. Even the best tools sometimes invent facts. Let us see how to catch mistakes before they cost you.

When an AI makes up a fact, a name, a number or a source, people call it a hallucination. It sounds confident, so it is easy to believe. You must learn to check.

Watch for red flags. Very exact numbers without a source. Links or books that you cannot find. Perfect answers about very recent events. And answers that change when you ask the same question again.

Use a three step check. Ask for the sources. Open them yourself, because the tool can invent a link. Then compare with a second, independent source before you decide.

Prefer official websites and documentation, original reports and data, and experts who sign their name. A random post on social media is not a source.

Be extra careful with money, health and legal topics, with prices and predictions, and with news from the last few days. In these cases, ask a real professional or use the official source.

Trust, but verify. This habit will protect you more than any tool. Next lesson: how to protect your privacy and your accounts while you use AI.
