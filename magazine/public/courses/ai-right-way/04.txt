[Slide 1 · 14s]
Lesson four. AI tools are useful, but they are also online services. In this lesson you will learn what never to share, and how to keep your accounts safe while you use them.

[Slide 2 · 15.6s]
A good rule is to treat every AI chat like a public place. Depending on the tool, your messages may be stored or reviewed. So never type anything that would hurt you if someone else read it.

[Slide 3 · 14.4s]
Never share passwords or one time codes, wallet recovery phrases and private keys, or ID numbers, bank cards and your home address. No honest service needs these things in a chat with an AI.

[Slide 4 · 13.2s]
Be careful with other people's data too. Do not paste private messages. Remove names and details before you ask for help, and ask permission before you upload someone's photo or document.

[Slide 5 · 15.6s]
Build safer habits. Use strong and different passwords, turn on two step login, and look at the privacy settings of each tool, such as chat history and whether your data can be used to improve the service.

[Slide 6 · 14.4s]
Beware of fake AI websites and apps. Copy cat apps can steal your login. Install apps only from official stores, and type the official address yourself instead of clicking links from messages or ads.

[Slide 7 · 14s]
At work or at school, follow the rules. Do not upload confidential documents or customer data to a public AI tool unless you are allowed to. When you are not sure, ask first.

[Slide 8 · 14.8s]
