[Slide 1 · 13.2s]
Welcome to Work Online the Smart Way, a free course from Jerry Development. In this first lesson we will look at what working online really is, and what it is not.

[Slide 2 · 15.6s]
Online work is real work. You trade a skill or your time for money, just like in any job. Anyone who promises easy money with no skills and no effort is selling a dream, or a scam.

[Slide 3 · 12s]
There are three common ways. Freelancing, where you sell a service to clients. Remote employment, where one company hires you. And selling your own products, content or courses.

[Slide 4 · 12s]
People pay for skills. Writing and translation. Design and video editing. Customer support. Programming and data work. And teaching or tutoring. Choose one and get good at it.

[Slide 5 · 14.4s]
To start you need one clear skill, a phone or a computer with internet, a simple profile with examples of your work, and patience. The first weeks are usually slow, and that is normal.

[Slide 6 · 12.4s]
Be realistic. Income takes time to build. Quality matters more than speed. And never pay money to get a job. Real employers pay you, they do not charge you.

[Slide 7 · 11.6s]
Your homework: choose one skill and practise it for thirty days. In the next lesson we will see which skills fit you and how to choose one.
