=== VOICE-OVER SCRIPT (read it, or paste it into a text-to-speech tool; slides are timed for a calm pace of about 150 words per minute) ===
[Slide 1 · 11.6s]
Lesson three. Clients do not know you. In less than one minute they decide if they trust you. Your profile and your portfolio must answer that question.

[Slide 2 · 14.4s]
A strong profile has a clear photo, your real name, and one sentence that says what you do and for whom. Add your languages and tools. Keep it simple and honest, with no exaggeration.

[Slide 3 · 10.8s]
No experience yet? Create your own samples. Choose three imaginary clients and do the work for them. Samples you made yourself are enough to start.

[Slide 4 · 14.8s]
Your portfolio needs three to five of your best samples. For each one, write a short description. What was the goal, and what did you do. And make sure it opens easily on a phone.

[Slide 5 · 12.4s]
Put it online in a free page or a simple website, and on your profile on freelance platforms. Keep one short link that you can send to any client.

[Slide 6 · 11.6s]
Never fake it. Do not copy other people's work, and do not invent clients or reviews. Honesty builds long term trust, and platforms close accounts that cheat.

[Slide 7 · 12s]
Show real work and explain it clearly. This week, create three samples and put them online. Next lesson: how to find your first client and write a proposal.
