🌴 Pi Oasis – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What we collect

  • Pi account identity: your Pi username and Pi user ID, provided by Pi Network when you sign in.
  • Game data: your farm, staking, scores, VIP status and referral information.
  • Payment records: Pi payment identifiers, amounts and transaction IDs for VIP subscriptions.
  • Referral link: the username of the player who invited you, if you joined through a referral link.
  • Fair-play signals: for Jerry Kombat and any future airdrop, how your game coins were earned, the days you played, tapping speed, and a one-way scrambled code made from your network address (the address itself is never stored). This only helps us spot automated or duplicate accounts.

We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts, wallet passphrase or any private keys.

How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress.
  • To process VIP subscriptions and credit referral rewards.
  • To show the weekly leaderboard (your Pi username and score are visible to other players).
  • To prevent cheating and abuse, including deciding who is eligible for a possible future Jerry airdrop.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Payment information is exchanged with Pi Network to complete payments. The game is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes requests to run the service.

Storage and deletion

Game data is stored on Cloudflare servers. Guests who don't sign in only have data on their own device. To ask for your data to be deleted, contact us through the Pi Developer Portal app page for Pi Oasis.

Children

Pi Oasis follows Pi Network's age requirements. It is not intended for children who are not allowed to use Pi Network.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
