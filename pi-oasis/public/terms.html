Last updated: November 11, 2026

The game

Pi Oasis is a farming and trading game. By playing you accept these terms and Pi Network's own terms.

Dates (in-game currency)

Dates 🪙 are an in-game currency only. They have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold, exchanged or withdrawn for Pi or any other currency. "Staking" in the Granary only uses dates.

Memberships

  • Bronze (0.25 Pi), Silver (0.5 Pi) and Gold (1 Pi) each last 30 days per payment and give in-game perks only (more staking slots and camels, higher staking and daily rewards, no ads, a ranking badge).
  • Upgrading converts the time left on your current membership by price. Payments are final once completed on the Pi blockchain, except where required by Pi Network rules.
  • The Partner membership is not for sale. Jerry Development grants it to its partners and may remove it.
  • Membership bonuses add dates but never count toward the weekly ranking score.

Weekly leaderboard prizes

  • Entry is free for every player. No purchase is needed to take part or win.
  • Prizes come from a share of the app's subscription revenue, never from other players' payments for entry.
  • Winners are the top 3 scores of the week (Monday to Sunday, UTC), sharing the pool 50% / 30% / 20%. Prizes are sent automatically to the winners' Pi wallets early the following week. If the pool is too small to cover network fees, it carries over to the next week. Prizes may be withheld for cheating or abuse.

Referrals

  • You earn 10% of what the app receives from the subscriptions of players you invited directly, while they stay subscribed. There is only one level.
  • Self-referrals and fake accounts are not allowed. Referral balances can be swapped for VIP or withdrawn from 1 Pi.

Fair play

Cheating, exploiting bugs, automation or multiple accounts may lead to losing progress, rewards or access.

Availability and liability

The game is provided "as is". We may change, pause or end features. We are not responsible for losses caused by network issues, Pi Network outages or events outside our control.
