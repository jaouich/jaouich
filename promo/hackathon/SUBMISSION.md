## App name
Oasis Farm Game (Pi Oasis)

## Short description (one line)
A free farming, racing and battle game for Pioneers: grow your oasis, race camels, fight bosses with the community, and win weekly Pi prizes.

## Description
Pi Oasis is a free mobile game made by the Jerry Development community for Pi Network. Players sign in with one tap through Pi Authentication and play right inside Pi Browser, in English, Arabic or French.

In the oasis, players plant crops, harvest dates (the in-game currency), raise camels, store dates in a granary for a bonus, and build their own city with houses, workshops and gardens. Daily events, daily missions and a Task center with daily, weekly and special tasks bring players back every day.

The app includes three games in one:
- **Oasis**: farm, camels and city building.
- **Jerry Kombat**: tap battles, upgrade cards, community bosses, leagues, tribes and limited collectibles.
- **Camel Derby**: train racing camels (speed, stamina, start), choose a tactic and race real players; 8 free races a day and weekly cups.

Every week, **100 players win**: the top 100 of the Oasis ranking share a prize pool in Pi, sent with App-to-User payments, plus in-game rewards. Only playing counts in the ranking: bought items never do, so the competition stays fair.

## Who it is for
Pioneers of every level who want a fun, fair game in their own language, where Pi has real, everyday use: from a 0.1 Pi pack to weekly Pi prizes.

## The problem it addresses
Many Pioneers hold Pi but have few places to use it in a simple, enjoyable way, and many new apps are only available in English. Pi Oasis gives Pi a real everyday use inside a game, rewards active players every week, and is fully translated into Arabic and French for large Pi communities in North Africa, the Middle East and Europe.

## How Pi is part of the experience
- **Pi Authentication**: one-tap sign-in, no password; the server verifies every access token with the Pi Platform API (`/me`).
- **Pi payments (User-to-App)**: memberships, level-up packs (from 0.1 Pi), JRY in-game currency, Derby feed and racing camels, founding supporter badges, business showcase listings. Every payment is approved and completed on our server.
- **App-to-User payments**: weekly prizes for the top 100, players' market sales and refunds.
- **Pi Ad Network**: rewarded ads (verified on our server with the Platform API) and light interstitial ads at natural breaks.
- **Notification Center**: weekly prize results, sold offers and a daily reminder, in the player's language, with deep links into the right page.
- **PiNet**: https://jerryoasis9559.pinet.com, with invite links that open the app directly.
- **Ecosystem**: the Testnet version is listed in the Pi Ecosystem with a 4.8 rating; the Mainnet version is submitted for listing.

## Technical notes
- Server-authoritative game logic (Cloudflare Workers + D1): every action is validated on the server, so the client can't cheat.
- 30+ automated test suites cover payments, payouts, rankings, ads verification, the Derby, tasks and notifications.
- Testnet progress carries over to Mainnet automatically on first sign-in.
- JRY is an in-game currency today, with a public roadmap toward Pi's official Launchpad (no investment offer, no promised value).
